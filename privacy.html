Privacy Policy – YOB Project Dock

YOB Project Dock does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

All data, including project names, paths, and settings, is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to any external servers.

The app does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

In-app purchases are handled securely by Apple through the App Store. No payment information is collected or stored by YOB Project Dock.

YOB Project Dock may open external applications based on user actions. No data is shared with these applications beyond what is required to open selected project folders.

If this policy changes in the future, updates will be reflected within the app and on this page.
