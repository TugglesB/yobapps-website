Privacy Policy – Blind Opera

Blind Opera does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

Blind Opera allows you to select music files stored on your device. Selected music is accessed and analyzed locally on your device solely to generate gameplay and visual effects. Your music files and information derived from their contents are not uploaded to Blind Opera or transmitted to external servers.

Gameplay information, including scores and settings, is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Blind Opera does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

Blind Opera does not require an account and does not collect payment information.

If this policy changes in the future, updates will be reflected on this page.
